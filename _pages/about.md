I am interested in reinforcement learning for humanoid robots,
with a focus on whole-body control, motion tracking,
and general-purpose locomotion policies.

My current work focuses on scalable teacher policy training
for large-scale humanoid motion tracking, including high-dynamic
motions and contact-rich behaviors.

My long-term goal is to build general-purpose humanoid control
policies that can serve as motor foundation models and interface
with vision-language-action systems.
